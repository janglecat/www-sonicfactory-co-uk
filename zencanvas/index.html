ZenCanvas

ZenCanvas is an app to play with art and sound.

It's simple, beautiful, and relaxing.

Features

  • Easy to play - Draw by tapping or clicking.
  • Generative visuals - Generative art with soft colours and smooth animations.
  • Generative music - Gentle sine wave audio created in realtime.
  • Completely free - No ads, no payment, no sign-up.

Download

Web - Play online in browser
PC - Download from itch.io

Screenshots

How to play

  • Tap & draw with your finger.
  • Press 'F' to toggle fullscreen mode.
  • Press 'Space' to pause and take screenshots for your desktop.
